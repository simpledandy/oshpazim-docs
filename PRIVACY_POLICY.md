# Privacy Policy for Oshpazim

**Last updated:** May 20, 2026

Ruxsoraxon ("we", "our", or "us") operates **Oshpazim** (the "Application"), a mobile application designed to search and filter traditional Uzbek recipes based on available pantry ingredients. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure and delightful experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application processes information. By using Oshpazim, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Oshpazim is designed as an offline-first application.** 

### 1.1 Personal Data
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address) to use the core features of the Application.
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you.

### 1.2 Local Storage (Room Database)
* **Pantry Ingredients:** Your chosen pantry ingredients, favorites, and onboarding preferences are stored locally on your device using a local Room SQLite database.
* **Data Persistence:** This data remains on your physical device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the application's data/cache in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the Application. We do not have access to this data.

### 1.3 Gemini AI Integration (Google AI Studio)
* **Optional AI Features:** If the Application utilizes on-device or server-side Gemini AI features, interactions (such as recipe-matching queries) may be processed securely through Google's Gemini API standard protocols.
* **API Key Management:** No user-owned credentials or API keys are collected or sent to our servers. Any API calls are routed through secure, direct API channels or local configuration blocks as provided by Google AI Studio's sandbox.

---

## 2. Permissions

The Application may request specific standard Android permissions:
* **Internet Access:** Used solely to fetch external recipe images (if any) or handle optional cloud API requests for Gemini AI matching.
* **Storage Access:** Used purely for internal caching and application state persistence.

You can modify or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's operating system settings.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party libraries and SDKs to ensure smooth operation:
* **Google Play Services:** Used for app store management and runtime environments.
* **Google AI / Gemini API:** Used to facilitate smart recipe parsing or query processing.
These third-party platforms apply their own privacy policies regarding how they handle request metadata.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since the Application does not collect *any* personal data, parents can rest assured that their children's privacy is secure.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

---
