# Oshpazim Privacy Policy / Maxfiylik siyosati

**[English](#en) · [O'zbekcha](#uz)**

<a id="en"></a>

## English

**Effective date:** October 25, 2026

Oshpazim is an Android app that suggests Uzbek recipes based on the ingredients and kitchen tools you have. It is made by Ruxsoraxon, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what choices you have.

### Summary

- No account, no sign-in, no ads.
- Your pantry, favorite recipes and shopping list are stored on your device.
- The app sends usage events, including what you search for, to our analytics provider PostHog, on servers in the European Union.
- Voice search uses the speech recognition service on your phone.
- We do not sell your data.

### 1. Information stored on your device

- Ingredients and kitchen tools you mark as available
- Favorite recipes
- Your shopping list, including items you type in yourself
- Settings, such as whether you finished the first-time setup and your preferred number of servings

This information stays in the app's private storage, and we cannot see it. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup under Google's terms. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it from your device.

### 2. Usage analytics (PostHog)

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used and to improve it. While you use the app, it sends events over an encrypted connection to PostHog servers in the European Union.

**What is sent**

- **Actions in the app**, for example: screens and tabs you open, recipes you view, favorites you add or remove, filters and sorting you choose, ingredients and kitchen tools you mark, the number of servings you choose, cooking-mode steps, timer use, shopping-list actions, pantry resets and completing the first-time setup.
- **Search text:** the search queries you type, and the text recognized from your speech when you use voice search.
- **Shopping-list item names** when you tick items off, including items you typed yourself.
- **Recipe and ingredient names** related to these actions (for example, which ingredients a recipe is missing).
- **App events:** the app being installed, updated, opened and moved to the background.
- **Device and app information:** device manufacturer and model, Android version, app version, screen size, language and time-zone settings, mobile carrier name, and whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
- **Approximate location:** when an event arrives, PostHog estimates your country, region and city from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored.
- **A random identifier** created on your device, used to recognise events that come from the same installation.

**What is not sent:** your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise (GPS) location, photos or audio recordings. The app does not use advertising identifiers, and analytics events are not linked to your identity.

Please don't type or say personal information in the search box. Whatever you search for is sent as described above.

**Why we process it (legal basis):** our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used, finding problems, and improving recipes and features.

**Who receives it:** PostHog, which processes the data on our behalf as a service provider ([PostHog privacy policy](https://posthog.com/privacy)). We do not sell or give this data to anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

**How long we keep it:** only as long as we need it to understand how the app is used; after that it is deleted.

### 3. Voice search

Voice search is optional. When you tap the microphone, the app asks for microphone permission (the first time only) and then uses the speech recognition service installed on your phone, usually Google's. That service needs an internet connection and processes your speech under its own privacy policy. The microphone is used only while the voice search window is open. Oshpazim does not record or keep any audio. The recognized text is used as your search query and is sent to analytics as described in section 2.

### 4. Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** to send analytics and to check for a connection before voice search.
- **Microphone:** only for voice search, requested the first time you use it. If you refuse, everything else in the app still works.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera or files. You can change permissions at any time in Android settings.

### 5. Your choices and rights

- The app does not currently have a setting to turn analytics off. To stop all data collection, uninstall the app. Clearing the app's data creates a new random identifier, so new events cannot be linked to earlier ones.
- Depending on where you live (for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation or the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data"), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, email us. Because analytics data is not linked to your name, we may not be able to tell which records are yours, but we will help as far as we reasonably can.

### 6. Children

Oshpazim is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it where we can.

### 7. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

### 8. Contact

Questions or requests: **kenjayevaruxsoraxon@gmail.com**

## O'zbekcha

**Kuchga kirgan sana:** 2026-yil 25-oktabr

Oshpazim — sizda bor masalliqlar va oshxona jihozlariga qarab o'zbek taomlari retseptlarini taklif qiladigan Android ilova. Uni mustaqil dasturchi Ruxsoraxon ("biz") yaratgan. Ushbu siyosat ilova qanday ma'lumotlar bilan ishlashini, ular qayerga yuborilishini va sizda qanday tanlov imkoniyatlari borligini tushuntiradi.

### Qisqacha

- Ro'yxatdan o'tish, hisob va reklama yo'q.
- Masalliqlaringiz, sevimli retseptlaringiz va bozorlik ro'yxatingiz qurilmangizda saqlanadi.
- Ilova foydalanish hodisalarini, jumladan qidiruv so'rovlaringizni, Yevropa Ittifoqidagi serverlarda ishlaydigan PostHog tahlil xizmatiga yuboradi.
- Ovozli qidiruv telefoningizdagi nutqni aniqlash xizmatidan foydalanadi.
- Biz ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz.

### 1. Qurilmangizda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

- Mavjud deb belgilagan masalliq va oshxona jihozlaringiz
- Sevimli retseptlaringiz
- Bozorlik ro'yxatingiz, jumladan o'zingiz yozib qo'shgan mahsulotlar
- Sozlamalar: boshlang'ich sozlashni tugatganingiz va tanlagan porsiya miqdoringiz

Bu ma'lumotlar ilovaning shaxsiy xotirasida saqlanadi, biz ularni ko'ra olmaymiz. Agar Google hisobingizda Android zaxira nusxasi (backup) yoqilgan bo'lsa, Android ularni Google shartlari asosida qurilma zaxirasiga qo'shishi mumkin. Ilova ma'lumotlarini tozalasangiz yoki ilovani o'chirsangiz, ular qurilmadan o'chiriladi.

### 2. Foydalanish tahlili (PostHog)

Ilovadan qanday foydalanilishini tushunish va uni yaxshilash uchun PostHog xizmatidan foydalanamiz. Ilovadan foydalanayotganingizda u hodisalarni shifrlangan aloqa orqali PostHog'ning Yevropa Ittifoqidagi serverlariga yuboradi.

**Nimalar yuboriladi**

- **Ilovadagi harakatlar**, masalan: ochilgan ekran va bo'limlar, ko'rilgan retseptlar, sevimlilarga qo'shish yoki olib tashlash, tanlangan filtr va saralash, belgilangan masalliq va jihozlar, tanlangan porsiya miqdori, pishirish rejimidagi bosqichlar, taymerdan foydalanish, bozorlik ro'yxatidagi amallar, masalliqlar ro'yxatini tozalash va boshlang'ich sozlashni tugatish.
- **Qidiruv matni:** yozgan qidiruv so'rovlaringiz va ovozli qidiruvda nutqingizdan aniqlangan matn.
- **Bozorlik ro'yxatidagi mahsulotlar nomi** — ularni belgilaganingizda, jumladan o'zingiz yozgan mahsulotlar.
- Shu harakatlarga oid **retsept va masalliq nomlari** (masalan, retseptda qaysi masalliqlar yetishmayotgani).
- **Ilova hodisalari:** ilovaning o'rnatilishi, yangilanishi, ochilishi va fonga o'tishi.
- **Qurilma va ilova haqida ma'lumot:** ishlab chiqaruvchi va model, Android versiyasi, ilova versiyasi, ekran o'lchami, til va vaqt mintaqasi sozlamalari, mobil operator nomi hamda Wi-Fi yoki mobil internetdan foydalanayotganingiz.
- **Taxminiy joylashuv:** hodisa kelganda PostHog IP manzilingiz asosida mamlakat, viloyat va shaharni taxminan aniqlaydi. IP manzilning o'zi saqlanmaydi.
- Qurilmangizda yaratiladigan **tasodifiy identifikator** — hodisalar bitta o'rnatilgan ilovadan kelganini bilish uchun.

**Nimalar yuborilmaydi:** ismingiz, elektron pochtangiz, telefon raqamingiz, kontaktlaringiz, aniq (GPS) joylashuvingiz, rasmlar yoki ovoz yozuvlari. Ilova reklama identifikatorlaridan foydalanmaydi, tahlil hodisalari esa shaxsingiz bilan bog'lanmaydi.

Iltimos, qidiruvda shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni yozmang yoki aytmang. Qidirgan narsangiz yuqorida aytilganidek yuboriladi.

**Nima uchun qayta ishlaymiz (huquqiy asos):** ilovadan qanday foydalanilishini tushunish, muammolarni topish hamda retseptlar va funksiyalarni yaxshilashdagi qonuniy manfaatimiz.

**Ma'lumotlarni kim oladi:** ularni biz uchun xizmat ko'rsatuvchi sifatida qayta ishlaydigan PostHog ([PostHog maxfiylik siyosati](https://posthog.com/privacy)). Biz bu ma'lumotlarni boshqa hech kimga sotmaymiz yoki bermaymiz va reklama uchun ishlatmaymiz.

**Qancha muddat saqlanadi:** faqat ilovadan qanday foydalanilishini tushunish uchun kerak bo'lgan muddatda, so'ng o'chiriladi.

### 3. Ovozli qidiruv

Ovozli qidiruv ixtiyoriy. Mikrofon tugmasini bosganingizda ilova (faqat birinchi marta) mikrofondan foydalanishga ruxsat so'raydi, so'ng telefoningizda o'rnatilgan nutqni aniqlash xizmatidan (odatda Google xizmatidan) foydalanadi. Bu xizmat internetni talab qiladi va nutqingizni o'z maxfiylik siyosati asosida qayta ishlaydi. Mikrofon faqat ovozli qidiruv oynasi ochiq turganda ishlatiladi. Oshpazim ovozni yozib olmaydi va saqlamaydi. Aniqlangan matn qidiruv so'rovi sifatida ishlatiladi va 2-bo'limda aytilganidek tahlilga yuboriladi.

### 4. Ruxsatlar

- **Internet va tarmoq holati:** tahlil ma'lumotlarini yuborish va ovozli qidiruvdan oldin internet borligini tekshirish uchun.
- **Mikrofon:** faqat ovozli qidiruv uchun, birinchi marta foydalanganingizda so'raladi. Rad etsangiz ham ilovaning qolgan barcha imkoniyatlari ishlayveradi.

Ilova joylashuv, kontaktlar, kamera yoki fayllarga ruxsat so'ramaydi. Ruxsatlarni istalgan vaqtda Android sozlamalarida o'zgartirishingiz mumkin.
